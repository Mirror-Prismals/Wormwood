Mirror (Prismals!)
Engine: Wormwood

The game takes place on a very large destructible planet. Ground appears locally flat; curvature becomes apparent at altitude. The world is not made of blocks. Realistic building is planned later.

Offset Geometric Contact will supply contact handling as part of future, separately tested simulations. Ground deformation, excavation, loose material and compaction require additional models and solvers; contact alone is not those simulations. No OGC or material simulation is implemented by this environment milestone.
