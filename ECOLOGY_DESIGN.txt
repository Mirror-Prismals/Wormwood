Mirror living terrain direction

The landscape, exposed geology and living cover must describe the same place. A
surface's material, slope, soil depth and moisture can guide suitable vegetation.
The current regional sampler supplies artistic descriptors for those quantities;
they are not measurements or validated soil and hydrology models.

Grass is rooted cover above a soil layer. Appearance can be instanced and varied
without making each blade a continuously simulated body. Nearby interaction needs
a separately bounded mechanical representation. Root mats can contribute cohesion
when the soil model can represent it. Pulling up turf, cutting vegetation and
removing roots require persistent material and plant state rather than cosmetic
deletion. Those mechanics are future work.

Trees need stable identities and authored species, trunk, branch, leaf and root
representations. Their placement must be deterministic and stable when a region
returns to view. Near interactions require explicit contact and damage handling.
Root support, bending, fracture, fallen wood and regrowth are separate capabilities
to add through contracted Systems. The current broadleaf meshes establish only
initial placement and appearance; they do not implement a forest simulation.

Clods are coherent aggregates of soil. Their mass and volume belong to the
material simulation. Plastic deformation, changing moisture, crumbling, merging
and compaction require a material model and accounted transfers between forms.
The new shovel trial uses a pre-separated elastic clod to study tool contact.
Decorative ground clods and the dynamic trial clod remain different declared
capabilities until persistent surface-to-material interaction is implemented.

High detail requires a distinction between visible detail and active mechanical
work, while preserving a common material identity. Distant plants can use cheaper
appearances; detailed mechanics can activate around interactions. Exact budgets,
render instancing, authored asset sets, spatial storage and activation criteria
remain to be designed and measured. New work must not introduce untracked native
rendering or physics paths, or enter the real-time audio callback.
